## What Went Well

- Checkout capability was built in a logical transaction flow across stories.
- Idempotent callback handling reduced operational risk in payment finalization.
- Order history/status visibility closed the loop for post-purchase customer confidence.

## Challenges Observed

- Payment callback reliability and duplicate-event handling required deep defensive logic.
- Cross-step pricing and state consistency demanded strict validation at boundaries.
- Regression risk was high due to intertwined cart, checkout, payment, and order domains.

## Key Lessons Learned

1. Idempotency controls are mandatory for any asynchronous payment lifecycle step.
2. Contract tests at integration boundaries prevent expensive checkout regressions.
3. Post-purchase visibility should be treated as part of checkout quality, not an afterthought.

## Technical Debt and Risks Carried Forward

- Additional resilience checks for external payment provider anomalies are still valuable.
- Operational monitoring around checkout failures and callback mismatches can be expanded.
