## What Went Well

- Story sequencing supported progressive discovery from listing to details and content.
- Search/filter and merchandising surfaces aligned well with product detail experience.
- CMS/blog consumption capability broadened customer engagement beyond transactional flows.

## Challenges Observed

- Filter combinations increased API/query complexity and test matrix size.
- Consistent ranking/ordering behavior required explicit deterministic rules.
- Content rendering and product data integration introduced cross-domain validation needs.

## Key Lessons Learned

1. Deterministic ordering must be explicit on all customer-facing lists.
2. Search/filter behavior should be validated with realistic high-variance datasets.
3. Discovery and content systems need shared fallback conventions for empty and partial states.

## Technical Debt and Risks Carried Forward

- Faceted search performance should be re-checked under larger catalog volume.
- Merchandising block strategy can benefit from tighter rule centralization.
