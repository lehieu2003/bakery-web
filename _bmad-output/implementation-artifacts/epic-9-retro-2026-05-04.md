- Regression safety improved:
  - Dispatcher and callback tests expanded to cover replay, multi-token fan-out, permanent token deactivation, and provider failure handling.

## Key Lessons Learned

1. Reliability work should be modeled as explicit story scope.
- Treating diagnostics and QA gate as first-class scope (9.3) prevented reliability from being deferred indefinitely.

2. Push channels need both delivery logic and audit logic.
- Delivery without structured diagnostics is insufficient for operations and support.

3. Idempotency contracts are reusable strategic assets.
- Reusing `NotificationDispatch.correlationKey` avoided duplicate state systems and reduced defect risk.

4. Status tracking must be hardened as process infrastructure.
- Sprint status and story status drift creates management noise and should be prevented by automated checks.
