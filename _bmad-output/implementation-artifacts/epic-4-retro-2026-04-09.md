## What Went Well

- Membership visibility and incentive application flowed naturally into checkout experiences.
- Quick reorder reduced friction for returning customers and aligned with order-history foundation.
- Coupon discovery and lifecycle hardening improved both conversion opportunities and policy integrity.

## Challenges Observed

- Incentive rule conflicts and precedence required careful central validation.
- Personalization relevance and fallback behavior needed clear guardrails.
- Coupon lifecycle complexity increased risk of policy drift without strict contract enforcement.

## Key Lessons Learned

1. Incentive and coupon engines need one source of truth for validation rules.
2. Personalization must degrade gracefully when signal quality is low.
3. Reorder acceleration is most stable when built on immutable order snapshots.

## Technical Debt and Risks Carried Forward

- Rule governance tooling can be improved to reduce manual policy verification.
- Analytics around personalization effectiveness should be expanded for tighter feedback loops.
